Common questions

How many explanations are free?

You get a set number of free explanations each month, shown on the Home screen and in Settings. They renew on the 1st of each month. The built-in examples, replies to a letter, and messages you write with Write in Czech don't use them up.

Is CzechPal official?

No. CzechPal is an independent app and isn't connected to the Czech government, the Ministry of the Interior, the police or any office. Office details, opening hours and checklists are copied from official websites, with the date we checked them and a link to the source.

Can I rely on the explanation?

Explanations and translations are written by AI and can contain mistakes. For dates, amounts, names and deadlines, the Czech original is what counts. CzechPal is not legal, medical or financial advice: for anything important, check with the office that wrote to you or with a professional.

Does CzechPal send anything for me?

Never. You review every reply and message, and you send it yourself by copying it or opening your SMS, WhatsApp or email app.

Which languages can I read explanations in?

English, Ukrainian, Russian, Vietnamese, Spanish, German, French and Polish. Change it in Settings. You can type what you want to say in Write in Czech in any language.

Where does the list of doctors come from?

From the official National Register of Healthcare Providers (Národní registr poskytovatelů zdravotních služeb, ÚZIS ČR), used under the CC BY 4.0 licence. Whether a practice takes new patients or speaks English is reported by other CzechPal users, with a date, and may be out of date: please confirm when you call.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete everything. It deletes your history and saved details on your phone and your anonymous account on our server, including any reports you sent. See the privacy policy for details.

Something looks wrong

If an office, an opening time or a practice is out of date, email us with what you saw and where. Thank you, it helps everyone.

In an emergency

Call 112 (operators also speak English) or 155 for an ambulance. CzechPal is not an emergency service.
