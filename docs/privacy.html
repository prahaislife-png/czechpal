CzechPal

Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

CzechPal explains Czech text and helps you write Czech. It's built to know as little about you as possible: no sign-up, no name, no email, no ads and no tracking.

In short

  • Your history, photos and saved details stay on your phone.
  • A text or photo leaves your phone only when you ask CzechPal to explain or write something. It goes to Anthropic's Claude AI to produce the answer, and we don't keep it.
  • The server only knows a random, anonymous account, used to count your free explanations.
  • You can delete everything, on your phone and on the server, in Settings.

What stays on your phone

Everything you scan, type or receive in the app is kept only on your device: photos and PDFs, the Czech text, explanations, replies and messages, your history, and the name, address and health insurer you can save in Write in Czech. We can't see any of it. Deleting the app, or Settings → Delete everything, removes it.

What we process on our server

1. An anonymous account

The first time you use a feature that needs the server, the app creates an anonymous account: a random ID with no name, email or phone number attached. It lets us count your free explanations each month and stop abuse.

2. What you ask us to explain or write

When you tap Explain, or ask CzechPal to write a reply or a message, the text, photos or PDF (and what you typed) are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the answer and sends the answer back to your phone.

  • We don't store this content. It isn't saved in our database or logs.
  • To make sure an answer lost to a bad connection can be retried without counting twice, we keep a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of the request. It can't be turned back into your text, is removed automatically once it's over a day old, and is deleted with your account.
  • Anthropic processes the content on our behalf to produce the answer. Under its commercial terms it does not use it to train its models. It may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in Anthropic's privacy policy.

The app asks for your permission before the first time anything is sent to the AI. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Send texts to the AI.

3. A usage log, without content

For each AI request we record the time, the kind of request (explanation, reply or message), the model used, its size, and whether it worked, linked to your anonymous account. It never includes what you sent or what came back. We use it to count your free explanations, limit how often requests can be made, keep costs under control and detect abuse. When you delete your account, these records lose the link to it.

4. Reports you choose to send

If you report on a doctor's practice (new patients, English, extra fees, insurers) or on waiting times at an immigration office, we store your answers, which are fixed choices with no free text, with your anonymous account, so each person counts once. Only combined summaries are shown to other users. Your reports are deleted when you delete your account.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or trackers, no selling or sharing of data for marketing.
  • No location tracking. The camera is used only while you scan.
  • No payments are processed in the app at the moment.

Legal bases

  • Providing the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): the anonymous account, explanations, replies and messages.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): sending your content to the AI. You can withdraw it in Settings.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): the usage log, rate limits and abuse prevention, and the reports you choose to send.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: hosting, database and server functions, in the EU (Ireland).
  • Anthropic: the Claude AI that writes explanations, replies and messages. Anthropic is based in the United States; transfers are covered by its data processing terms and the safeguards they include.

Apple and Google distribute the app under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

  • Content you ask us to explain or write: not stored by us.
  • Your anonymous account, its monthly count and your reports: until you delete your account in the app.
  • The usage log: as long as needed for cost accounting and abuse prevention, without the link to you once your account is deleted.

Your rights

You can delete all your data yourself in Settings → Delete everything. You also have the right to access, correct or restrict your data, to object, and to withdraw consent. Because your account is anonymous, we can only find your data through the app, so please contact us from the app's Help page. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů.

Children

CzechPal is not meant for children under 16.

Security

All connections are encrypted. The database only accepts requests from our server functions, and no user can read another user's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
