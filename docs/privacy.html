CzechLocal

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

CzechLocal explains Czech text and helps you write Czech. It's built to know as little about you as possible: no sign-up, no name, no email, no ads and no tracking.

In short

  • Your history, photos, saved details and voice recordings stay on your phone.
  • A text or photo leaves your phone only when you ask CzechLocal to explain or write something, or to give feedback on your writing practice. It goes to Anthropic's Claude AI to produce the answer, and we don't keep it.
  • If you talk with CzechLocal in Speak Czech, your voice goes live to OpenAI, which runs the conversation, and the text of it to Anthropic's Claude for meanings and feedback. Nothing is recorded or kept by us.
  • The server only knows a random, anonymous account, used to count your free explanations.
  • You can delete everything, on your phone and on the server, in Settings.

What stays on your phone

Everything you scan, type or receive in the app is kept only on your device: photos and PDFs, the Czech text, explanations, replies and messages, your history, the name, address and health insurer you can save in Write in Czech, what you tell CzechLocal in About you (your type of residence permit, its expiry date and your district), your reminders, your progress in Learn and A2 Academy (texts read, saved words and phrases, your plan and streak, your answers, what you write in practice and your speaking recordings) and the places you save in Explore. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself, not by us. We can't see any of it. Deleting the app, or Settings → Delete everything, removes it.

Your location

If you allow it, Explore uses your location to show where you are on the map and list places near you, nearest first. It's used on your phone only and never sent to us. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings; Explore then shows the whole city. The map itself comes from Apple Maps on iPhone and Google Maps on Android, under their own privacy policies; Directions opens their app.

Your microphone

If you allow it, speaking practice in A2 Academy records your voice so you can play your answers back. The microphone is on only while you record an answer, and a recording stops by itself after two minutes. Recordings are saved on your phone only, one for each question (a new one replaces the old), and are never sent to us or to the AI. You can delete them at the end of each task, with Settings → Delete everything, or by deleting the app. You can turn the microphone off at any time in your phone's settings; speaking practice then works without recording.

In Speak Czech, the microphone is on only during a conversation you start, and it goes off when the conversation ends, when you leave the app, or after a long silence. What you say is streamed to the AI as described below; it is not recorded on your phone or on our server. You can turn the microphone off during a conversation, or type instead.

What we process on our server

1. An anonymous account

The first time you use a feature that needs the server, the app creates an anonymous account: a random ID with no name, email or phone number attached. It lets us count your free explanations each month and stop abuse.

2. What you ask us to explain or write

When you tap Explain or Translate, ask CzechLocal to write a reply or a message, or ask for feedback on your writing practice in A2 Academy, the text, photos or PDF (and what you typed) are sent over an encrypted connection, together with your About you answers if you gave any, so the explanation fits your situation, to our server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the answer and sends the answer back to your phone.

  • We don't store this content. It isn't saved in our database or logs.
  • To make sure an answer lost to a bad connection can be retried without counting twice, we keep a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of the request. It can't be turned back into your text, is removed automatically once it's over a day old, and is deleted with your account.
  • Anthropic processes the content on our behalf to produce the answer. Under its commercial terms it does not use it to train its models. It may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in Anthropic's privacy policy.

The app asks for your permission before the first time anything is sent to the AI. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Send texts to the AI.

3. Speak Czech conversations

When you start a conversation in Speak Czech, the app connects your microphone directly to OpenAI's voice AI (GPT-Live) over an encrypted connection; our server only sets the connection up, with our key, and never hears the audio. OpenAI turns your speech into text and answers in Czech. With the conversation, the app sends a few facts from your progress on your phone so the partner can pitch it for you: a level from 0 to 5, up to six Czech words you're learning, one practice goal, and your explanation language. If you choose to see meanings, type instead, or get feedback at the end, the text of the conversation goes through our server to Anthropic's Claude API.

  • No recording is made or kept, on your phone or by us, and we don't store the conversation's text. Only the feedback and short quotes of your own words that it uses as evidence of your progress are saved, on your phone.
  • OpenAI processes your voice and the conversation on our behalf. We ask it not to store the session. Under its API terms it does not use API data to train its models; it may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • For each voice conversation we record when it started and ended, the situation and how many seconds it used, linked to your anonymous account, to give everyone a fair daily speaking time, end conversations that run too long, and keep costs under control.

The app asks for your permission before your first conversation. CzechLocal doesn't assess pronunciation.

4. A usage log, without content

For each AI request we record the time, the kind of request (explanation, reply, message, translation, writing feedback, or a Speak Czech meaning, typed reply or feedback), the model used, its size, and whether it worked, linked to your anonymous account. It never includes what you sent or what came back. We use it to count your free explanations, limit how often requests can be made, keep costs under control and detect abuse. When you delete your account, these records lose the link to it.

5. Reports you choose to send

If you report on a doctor's practice (new patients, English, extra fees, insurers) or on waiting times at an immigration office, we store your answers, which are fixed choices with no free text, with your anonymous account, so each person counts once. Only combined summaries are shown to other users. Your reports are deleted when you delete your account.

6. Public lists the app downloads

Some parts of the app download public information from our server: places in Explore (from OpenStreetMap), and doctors, pharmacies and offices in Find (from official registers). These requests carry no personal data beyond what any internet connection does, and we don't link them to you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics or trackers, no selling or sharing of data for marketing.
  • No location tracking: your location, if you allow it, stays on your phone. The camera is used only while you scan, and the microphone only while you record a speaking answer or talk in a Speak Czech conversation.
  • No payments are processed in the app at the moment.

Legal bases

  • Providing the service you ask for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): the anonymous account, explanations, replies and messages.
  • Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): sending your content, or your voice in Speak Czech, to the AI. You can withdraw it in Settings, or simply not use these features.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): the usage log, rate limits and abuse prevention, and the reports you choose to send.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: hosting, database and server functions, in the EU (Ireland).
  • Anthropic: the Claude AI that writes explanations, replies and messages. Anthropic is based in the United States; transfers are covered by its data processing terms and the safeguards they include.
  • OpenAI: the voice AI behind Speak Czech conversations. OpenAI is based in the United States; transfers are covered by its data processing terms and the safeguards they include.

Apple and Google distribute the app under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

  • Content you ask us to explain or write: not stored by us.
  • Your voice in Speak Czech: not recorded or stored by us.
  • Your anonymous account, its monthly count and your reports: until you delete your account in the app.
  • The Speak Czech session log (times, situation, seconds): like the usage log below.
  • The usage log: as long as needed for cost accounting and abuse prevention, without the link to you once your account is deleted.

Your rights

You can delete all your data yourself in Settings → Delete everything. You also have the right to access, correct or restrict your data, to object, and to withdraw consent. Because your account is anonymous, we can only find your data through the app, so please contact us from the app's Help page. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů.

Children

CzechLocal is not meant for children under 16.

Security

All connections are encrypted. The database only accepts requests from our server functions, and no user can read another user's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
