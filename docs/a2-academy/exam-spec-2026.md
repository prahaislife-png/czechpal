## Structure and timing

| Part | Time | Points | Rule source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reading (čtení) | 40 min | 25 (25 items, 1 point each) | S1, S3 p.10, p.17 |
| Writing (psaní) | 25 min | 20 (Task 1: 8, Task 2: 12) | S1, S3 p.10, p.67, p.87–89 |
| Break | 15 min | | S3 p.10 |
| Listening (poslech) | about 35–40 min (as long as the recording) | 25 (25 items, 1 point each) | S1, S3 p.10, p.91 |
| Speaking (mluvení) | at most 15 min | 40 (37 for the tasks + 3 for pronunciation) | S1, S3 p.10, p.121, p.140 |

Reading and writing are written together without a break (S3 p.10). The oral part is the same day,
at a time set by the system (S3 p.10).

## Passing

- Reading + writing + listening together: **at least 42 of 70** (60 %). (S1; S3 p.91; S5 p.2)
- Speaking: **at least 24 of 40** (60 %). (S1; S5 p.2)
- Both are needed. Failing either means repeating the whole exam. (S5 p.2: "Když nezískáte minimum
  bodů jak v písemné, tak ústní zkoušce, musíte celou zkoušku opakovat.")
- A retake is possible at the earliest 3 months later, at most 3 times a year; the result is valid
  without limit; the certificate is the "Osvědčení o znalosti českého jazyka", sent within 30 days. (S3 p.10–11)

## Reading: 5 tasks, 25 items

| Task | Items | Points | Format (S5 instructions; S3 p.17) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–5 | 5 | Match answer and text ("spojit odpověď a text") |
| 2 | 6–10 | 5 | One text; choose one answer A–D for each item |
| 3 | 11–14 | 4 | Several short texts and information about people; choose a suitable person A–E for each text; one person is not needed |
| 4 | 15–20 | 6 | Short texts; choose the right option A–D for each (word choice) |
| 5 | 21–25 | 5 | Read a text and write one piece of information for each item (open answer) |

Each task starts with a worked example (item 0). Answers go on an answer sheet; one cross per row,
two crosses make the answer wrong. (S3 p.16–17)

## Writing: 2 tasks, 20 points

**Task 1 – Form (formulář), 8 points.** Answer 3 questions in a form, each in full sentences,
**at least 10 words per answer**. Each answer must address the question word (kde, kdy, proč…),
start with a capital letter, end with a full stop, and contain a verb in the correct form.
**A shorter answer loses points** ("Za každou kratší odpověď ztratíte body") — not zero for the task.
(S3 p.66, p.87; S4 p.1)

**Task 2 – E-mail, 12 points.** Write an e-mail/letter of **at least 35 words** following the
instruction (the underlined word says the type: apology, invitation, greeting, request…) and the
pictures. Required: a correct opening and closing, a signature (any name), at least one sentence of
the requested type, and at least one sentence answering the question at **each** picture (the model
has 5 pictures, so at least 5 sentences). Use ty/vy as the situation requires.
**Fewer than 35 words: 0 points for Task 2.** (S3 p.66–67, p.88–89; S4 p.1)

## Listening: 5 tasks, 25 items, each recording heard twice

| Task | Items | Format (S5 instructions) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–5 | Five dialogues; choose one answer A–D for each |
| 2 | 6–10 | Five texts; choose one answer A–D for each; 1 minute to read the items first |
| 3 | 11–15 | Five texts; choose from A–G; two options are extra; 15 s to look at the table |
| 4 | 16–20 | Five dialogues; choose the right picture A–F; one picture is extra; 15 s to look at the pictures |
| 5 | 21–25 | A voice message; write one piece of information for each item; 1 minute to read the items first |

The recording runs the whole part and warns 5 minutes before the end. (S3 p.91; S5)

## Speaking: 4 tasks, 40 points

| Task | Points | What happens (S3 p.121, p.137–140; S4 p.2–3) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 – Answers to questions | 8 | 8 questions, 4 on each of 2 everyday topics (housing, travel, food, shopping, weather, work, free time, education, health…). The topics are known, the questions are not. Answer straight away, in a full sentence with a verb, matching the question word and the tense of the question. |
| 2 – Dialogues | 12 | 2 dialogues with the examiner (e.g. a shop, the street, an office). In each, ask 4 questions: 3 from the card's prompts and 1 of your own. Formal "vy". Correct question form matters ("V kolik hodin…", not "V který čas…"). |
| 3 – Story from pictures | 10 | Tell what people did yesterday from 4 pictures, at least one sentence per picture, **in the past tense**, about the people in the pictures (not about yourself). |
| 4 – Solving a situation | 7 | Agree with the examiner, playing a friend (informal "ty"), on 1 activity, place or thing out of the 3 in the pictures. Offer each picture; explain why you chose the last one. Only what is in the pictures. |
| Pronunciation | 3 | Across the whole oral part. |

There is no preparation time ("Na otázky odpovídáte při ústní zkoušce hned"); the oral part lasts at
most 15 minutes; one examiner asks, two others assess. (S3 p.121)

## Not verified (not encoded as exam rules)

- **A per-answer time limit in speaking** (the brief mentions 7 seconds): not found in S1–S5. The app
  must not enforce or advertise one.
- **A time limit for the picture story** (the brief mentions one minute): not found.
- **The detailed marking scales** (how the 8/12/10/7 points are split by criteria): S3 states what is
  required, not the scale. AI marks for writing and speaking are therefore **estimates**, and the app
  says so.
- **The exact format of reading task 1**: S3 says it is matching ("spojit odpověď a text"); the full
  instruction was not legible in the extracted text of S5.
